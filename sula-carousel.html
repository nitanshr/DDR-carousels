The founder
Rajeev Samant

He didn’t just make Indian wine. He made India believe in it.

Rajeev Samant left Silicon Valley to plant grapes in Nashik in the late 90s, in a country where almost no one drank wine. Twenty-five years on, Sula is India’s largest wine producer and what it calls the world’s most-visited vineyard, over 3 lakh guests a year.

He named it Sula after his mother, Sulabha. It was personal from the first bottle.

Pour 01 · White
The Source Series
100% Sauvignon Blanc

The Source Sauvignon Blanc.

A dry, oak-aged Sauvignon Blanc from select estate vineyards. Full of tropical and green fruit, partially barrel-fermented and partly aged in French oak.

Pour 02 · White
Reserve
100% Chenin Blanc

Chenin Blanc Reserve.

Sula’s best-selling white. Off-dry, so a touch of sweetness (around 16 to 18 g of sugar), rich and lush with a lingering vanilla note from the oak. Pear, pineapple and citrus.

Pour 03 · Rosé
The Source Series
100% Grenache

The Source Grenache Rosé.

Rich, lush and well-rounded, with real complexity for a rosé. Citrus, tropical fruit and white peach. Rajiv’s pick for India’s best rosé, and the first of the Source series.

Grenache didn’t sing as a red here, so they turned it into a rosé. The constraint made the wine.

Pour 04 · Red
The Source Series
100% Cabernet Sauvignon

The Source Cabernet Sauvignon.

Smooth and fresh, with aromas of cherry and blackberry leading into plum and chocolate. Well-balanced, with refreshing acidity and a memorable finish. The grapes have to be fully ripe, or the colour turns green.

@DineDrinkRepeat06 / 08
Pour 05 · Red
Rasa
95% Syrah, 5% Viognier

Rasa Syrah.

Dark and red berries, vanilla, mocha and spice. Half aged a year in French oak, half not, then blended, so it stays elegant rather than oaky. Take a big sip and chew it.

Syrah and Shiraz are the same grape. Shiraz goes big at 14 to 15%; this leans elegant and restrained. That’s the whole personality.
