The comparison
04

Ube is not actually “the new matcha.”

Matcha

A ritual.

  • Built on caffeine
  • Bitter, grassy, savoury
  • Whisked, prepared, performed
  • Wears a wellness identity
Ube

An indulgence.

  • No caffeine on its own
  • Sweet, earthy, nutty
  • Poured, layered, photographed
  • Built for dessert

They may share shelf space, but they don’t solve the same craving.
