The context
食パン

Delhi is having a sandwich revolution. Shokupan is out front.

Named after Japanese milk bread, it bakes that soft, cloud-like loaf in-house every day, and makes every sauce and spread fresh. The counter in Basant Lok now has a small, cosy corner to actually sit in.

Pick-up heavy until now, but worth lingering.

The verdict
食パン

If Delhi’s sandwich moment has a forerunner, this is it.

Great bread, made in-house, treated seriously. Now with a corner to sit and enjoy it in. Go for the honey toast, stay for everything else.
