02 of 04 · Shenzhen04
Bar Mood
The independent wildcard of the crawl. Dive-bar pricing and mood, with a serious tea-driven cocktail list.
Mountain Essence
Jin Xuan tea, bourbon whisky, camellia. Earthy and mellow.
Presents of God
Earl Grey tea, gin, plum wine, cardamom. The bitterness kept well in check.

03 of 04 · Guangzhou05
DSK Cocktail Club
A multi-floor playground, laid out like a hotel: lounge, room service and a suite, each floor a different mood.
Matcha Gimlet
Gin, matcha, magnolia, lime, flower hydrosol. One of the better matcha cocktails I’ve had.
Taco High
Doritos-infused tequila, pico de gallo, soda, avocado-nacho garnish. Spicy, savoury, unforgettable.

04 of 04 · Shenzhen06
SanYou
I arrived late and didn’t catch a cocktail. But the concept is worth knowing: a bar built to rethink Chinese spirits.
Balang Gin
A Chinese botanical gin. SanYou sources its spirits from across the mainland.
Baijiu
China’s national spirit, reimagined as a cocktail base rather than a neat dinner pour.
